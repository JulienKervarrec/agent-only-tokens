Registry monitor

Reads an AttestedAgentRegistry on Base Sepolia straight from public nodes, in your browser, with no server: who is an agent, which verifier is in force, which decisions are announced and when they take effect, and which venues are approved. Read-only: it signs nothing and asks for no wallet.

Nodes used, in order: . Events are read in ranges of blocks from the registry's deployment block. A registry of an older version may lack some functions: they show as "—". Part of Agent-Only Tokens, test network only.
